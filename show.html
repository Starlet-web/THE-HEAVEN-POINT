Frequently Asked Questions

How many seats are available?
Total 15 seats across 5 rows per show. Each row has 3 seats. First 3 rows are Regular (₹50), last 2 rows are Premium (₹70).
When does the booking close?
Booking closes 1 hour before the show time or when all seats are booked, whichever is earlier.
How will I get my ticket?
After payment, a digital ticket with QR code will be sent via WhatsApp and email. Show it at the entry.
Can I cancel my booking?
Cancellations are allowed up to 2 hours before the show. Contact us on WhatsApp for support.
